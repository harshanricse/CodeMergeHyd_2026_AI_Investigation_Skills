Summary: Discounted orders are taxed on the full subtotal instead of the discounted amount. Root cause: [TaxCalculator.cs](../source/order-service/TaxCalculator.cs#L16-L21) ignores the discount parameter and uses `subtotal * taxRate`; pricing rules require tax on `subtotal - discount`. Evidence: `TaxCalculator ... subtotal=100.00 discount=20.00 taxRate=0.10 tax=10.00` and the receipt shows `total=90.00`; expected is `tax=8.00` and `total=88.00`. Suggested fix: calculate tax on the discounted subtotal and add a regression test. Confidence: High.
